Common topics

  • Notifications are not firing
  • Widgets are not refreshing
  • Apple Watch sync issues

Prayer times look wrong

Check that your location permission is enabled and that your selected city or mosque is correct. If the app offers multiple calculation methods or local mosque-based times, make sure the right source is selected.

Notifications are not working

Open iPhone Settings and confirm notifications are allowed for Muslim360. Also make sure Focus modes, Silent mode, or scheduled summaries are not preventing alerts from appearing.

Widgets are not updating

Try removing the widget and adding it again. Widgets may also refresh based on system timing rather than instantly. Make sure the app has opened recently and location access is still enabled if needed.

Apple Watch app is not syncing

Open the iPhone app and the Watch app once to help sync fresh data. Also confirm Bluetooth is on, the watch is paired correctly, and both devices are using the latest available software.
