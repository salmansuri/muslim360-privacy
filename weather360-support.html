Common topics

  • Weather notifications not firing
  • Rain timing looks incorrect
  • Widgets are not updating
  • Live Activity is not refreshing
  • Apple Watch sync issues

Notifications are not appearing

Check that notifications are enabled for Weather360 in iPhone Settings. Also review Focus modes, Scheduled Summary, and whether alerts are allowed on the Lock Screen.

Minute cast or rain timing looks off

Weather data can change quickly as forecast models update. Open the app to refresh the latest data and confirm the selected location is correct.

Widgets are stale or not refreshing

Try opening the app once, then remove and re-add the widget if needed. iOS controls widget refresh timing, so updates may not happen instantly in the background.

Live Activity is not updating

Make sure Live Activities are enabled in iPhone Settings for Weather360. If the app has not refreshed recently, reopening it can help re-sync the current weather state.

Apple Watch is not showing the latest weather

Open both the iPhone app and the Watch app once to encourage a fresh sync. Also confirm Bluetooth is on, the watch is paired correctly, and both devices are updated.
